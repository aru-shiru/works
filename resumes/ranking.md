# Ranking

Do not attach. This file is the order. A GitHub profile, lamacca.com, a personal site, or a CV takes from here — they do not set it.

## How we rank

Higher number loses to an earlier rule, even if the client is famous or the year is newer.

1. **Still shipping on the stack you still sell** (Nest/Postgres, Laravel/RDS, Expo/Nuxt) beats shipped-and-left.
2. **You as sole engineer or hands-on tech lead** beats review, pair-split, or “Nana drove.”
3. **Scale and constraint** (members migrated, row cutover, tenants, dinas) beats a one-off event or a lost clone.
4. **One story per shape.** Two Livewire rewrites, two Kaltara catalogs, or two “I led Bimo and Nana on Laravel” lines do not sit in the top consecutive ranks. The stronger one stays; the other drops.
5. **A distinct surface** (native/Japan, Flutter field, alumni) still ranks, but under the current full-stack production set.
6. **Same client, keep together.** Kaltara is a block: longest/heaviest first.
7. **Evidence.** Git and a running system beat a dummy Hosting box with no clone.
